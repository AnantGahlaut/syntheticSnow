SyntheticSnow · CryoGARS, Boise State University

A physics snow model, tested against real snow

SyntheticSnow simulates a whole winter of mountain snow on a 30 m grid, from the terrain, the trees and the daily weather: snowfall, trees catching snow, wind moving snow, compaction, melt and sublimation. It will be used to make realistic training data for a neural network that maps snow from radar. These pages compare the model with measurements it never saw. Nothing in the model is tuned to them.

LiDAR map test · 30 m → Model snow-depth maps vs airborne LiDAR at 9 SnowEx site-dates (Idaho, Colorado, Utah; 2020-2021) and at every NIVAL flight over Mores Creek, Idaho, in winter 2026 (about every 12 days, December to April), plus a 12-day forecast test. SNOTEL station test → The model through the whole winter at 16 SNOTEL station-winters, run on each station's own weather, compared every day with the snow pillow (SWE) and sonic depth sensor.
Data: NASA SnowEx and NIVAL airborne LiDAR (NSIDC DAAC), USDA NRCS SNOTEL, gridMET. Interactive 3D views need a browser with WebGL2.
